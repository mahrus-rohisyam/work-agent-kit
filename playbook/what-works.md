# What works

Craft lessons that apply to every brand, from the user's feedback (`/retro`) and from research
(`/refresh`). Newest first. Each entry: date, lesson, source (feedback / campaign results / URL), evidence level.

## Starting defaults (opinion, to be tested)
- Hook = the result or the promise, on screen in the first 1.5 s.
- 2–6 words per caption chunk, at least 0.9 s each.
- One feature per video. A second feature is a second video.
- End with one next step, not three.
